## Verification Rules

Before publishing an image commercially:

1. Locate the original image page or obtain confirmation from the photographer.
2. Record the source URL and creator.
3. Check the exact license and whether commercial use is allowed.
4. Record any required attribution, license link, or modification notice.
5. If permission cannot be established, replace the image with one whose rights can be verified.

**Important:** Finding a visually similar image online does not establish the provenance or license of the local file.
